These pages are regenerated from the current generator on every push. The subject is a toy shop: a layered Python backend, a TypeScript client and a feature/add-refunds branch with its plan.

What a reviewer approves at design sign-off: the declared files, planned interfaces and planned flows.

Three problems are planted in the branch for the page to find:

  • A layer violation. models.py imports a constant from the service layer above it; the edge is red.
  • Unplanned. money.py is not in the plan, so it is amber in Diff mode.
  • Missing. The plan promises emails.py, but nobody wrote it; it is drawn red and dashed.
